About Looc

Looc is a coastal municipality located in the southern part of Tablas Island, the largest island in the province of Romblon, Philippines. It is part of the MIMAROPA Region and is known for its natural resources, agricultural activities, fishing industry, local culture, and growing tourism potential. More than a destination for travelers, Looc is a community where people live, work, study, conduct business, and preserve their local traditions. Its coastal location and surrounding hills contribute to its distinctive landscape and provide important resources for the livelihood of its residents.
